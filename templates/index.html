HOW PIXELLE WORKS

From thousands of photos
to your moments.

Designed for college events, celebrations, fests and gatherings where one photographer can capture hundreds of memories.

↑

Upload the event

Create an event and upload the photographs captured by your photographer. Pixelle organizes them into a private event gallery.

◉

Faces are indexed

OpenCV detects faces inside the uploaded photographs and creates numerical face representations for searching.

✦

Find your moments

Upload a clear selfie and Pixelle compares your face representation with the indexed event photos to find potential matches.
